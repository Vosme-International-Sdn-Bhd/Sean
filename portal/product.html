The award-winning Moroccanoil Treatment is a universal multi-use product that can be used as a conditioning, styling and finishing tool. Infused with argan oil, this lightweight, non-greasy formula detangles hair, boosts shine and conditions from root to tip.

Key Benefits

  • Conditions, detangles and strengthens hair
  • Boosts shine and smooths frizz
  • Speeds up drying time by up to 40%
  • Builds hair's natural resilience over time
  • Suitable for all hair types

About the Brand

Moroccanoil is a professional salon brand distributed in over 60 countries, known for introducing argan oil to the beauty industry. Used and trusted by professional stylists at Precision Salon outlets nationwide.

Certifications

  • Suitable for Colour-Treated Hair
  • Silicone-Free Formula (Light version)
  • Dermatologically Tested
  • No Animal Testing

Application — Styling

  • Apply 1–2 pumps to damp hair before blowdrying or styling
  • Work evenly through mid-lengths to ends
  • Style as desired — blowdry, curl, or straighten

Application — Finishing

  • Apply a small amount to dry, styled hair to eliminate frizz and add shine
  • Start with a small amount — a little goes a long way

Application — Scalp Nourishment

  • For deep conditioning, apply to dry hair from mid-length to ends
  • Leave for 10–15 minutes, then shampoo as normal

Pro Tip

Layer with Moroccanoil Hydrating Shampoo and Hydrating Mask for amplified results. Available in our outlet and online store.
